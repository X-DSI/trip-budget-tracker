## Goal
Anyone on the trip can log an expense in under 10 seconds, with or without signal, and split it
the way the group agrees: equally per family, per head, dish by dish, custom amounts, or personal.

## Scope
**In:**
- **Expenses tab:** expenses grouped by day (newest first), each showing title, category,
  amount, who paid (family colour), split type, and who added or last edited it. Totals for today
  and for the trip. A pending-sync marker on unsynced expenses.
- **Add/edit sheet:**
  - amount in rupees, converted once with `toPaise`;
  - title, category chips (fuel, tolls, food, stay, tickets, shopping, transport, other);
  - paid by, defaulting to the user's family;
  - date, defaulting to today in the trip's timezone;
  - split mode, defaulting to the last one used on this device, with a live preview of each
    family's share from `splitExpense`;
  - participants, defaulting to all active families;
  - optional note.
- **Split editors:**
  - **dish by dish:** add items with an amount and "who had it" (a family or shared), plus
    extras (tax, service, tip); shows the remaining amount until items + extras equal the bill;
  - **custom:** one amount per family, with the remaining amount shown live;
  - the sheet refuses to save until the split adds up.
- **Edit and delete** (soft delete) for the creator and organisers only.
- **Firestore rules** for `expenses` per the access table and the who-can-change-money ADR,
  validating fields: amount bounds, mode enum, `createdBy` fixed, `deleted` bool.
- **Dashboard:** fill the during-trip "Today's spend" card (total today, and the user's family's
  share).

**Out:** Receipt photos, OCR, currencies other than INR.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Rules: members add expenses as themselves; only the creator or an organiser can edit or delete; nobody changes `createdBy`; non-members can't read; bad amounts and modes are rejected — check: `npm run test:rules`
- [ ] Unit: the form-to-expense mapping produces exact paise for every mode, and refuses itemized or custom splits that don't add up — check: `npm test`
- [ ] E2E: add a ₹4,000 per-head dinner for families of 2/4/3/3 adults and see 666.67 / 1,333.33 / 1,000 / 1,000 previewed and saved — check: `npm run test:e2e -- --grep @expenses`
- [ ] E2E @offline: an expense added offline appears at once with a pending marker and syncs when back online — check: `npm run test:e2e -- --grep @offline`
- [ ] E2E: a member can't edit another member's expense; an organiser can — check: `npm run test:e2e -- --grep @expenses`
- [ ] Adding an expense takes under 10 seconds on a phone — check: manual

## Constraints
- All split maths goes through `src/domain` (`splitExpense`, `toPaise`); no new money arithmetic in
  UI code.
- Never `await` an expense write in the UI; use the offline queue.

## Implementer may decide
Layout of the sheet, how items are entered, category icons, list grouping details.

## Must escalate
Changes to the split semantics or the data model beyond what's listed in `docs/data-model.md`.
